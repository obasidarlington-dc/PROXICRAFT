📜 Terms and Conditions

1. Eligibility. Users must be at least 18 years of age and reside in Nigeria to register.

2. Artisan Qualification. All artisans must hold a minimum SSCE, WAEC, NECO, or equivalent qualification. Registration with false qualification information leads to immediate account suspension.

3. Role Restriction. Artisans may only apply for jobs matching their registered trade category. Attempting to circumvent this is a violation of these terms.

4. Unique Identity. Each email address and username may be used for only one account. A person may not register as both artisan and client.

5. Accurate Information. Users must provide accurate and truthful information. Misrepresentation, fraud, or deception results in permanent account termination.

6. Conduct. Harassment, threats, abuse, or discriminatory behaviour will result in immediate account suspension.

7. Reporting. Users who observe misconduct should report it through the platform's report function. False or malicious reports are also a violation.

8. Admin Oversight. The ProxiCraft admin team may review all accounts and suspend or permanently delete any account that violates these terms, without prior notice.

9. Account Deletion. Users may delete their accounts at any time from their profile. All associated data will be permanently removed. Deleted accounts cannot be recovered.

10. Data and Privacy. Personal data collected during registration is stored securely and used only to operate the platform.

11. Limitation of Liability. ProxiCraft is a matching platform only and does not guarantee employment or quality of work.

12. Amendments. ProxiCraft may amend these terms at any time. Continued use constitutes acceptance of updated terms.
