📜 Terms and Conditions

1. Eligibility. Users must be at least 18 years of age and reside in Nigeria to register.

2. Artisan Qualification. Artisans must hold a minimum SSCE/WAEC qualification. False information leads to suspension.

3. Role Restriction. Artisans may only apply for jobs matching their registered trade category.

4. Unique Identity. Each email and username may only be used for one account. You cannot register as both artisan and client.

5. Accurate Information. All information must be truthful. Misrepresentation leads to account termination.

6. Conduct. Harassment, threats, or abuse results in immediate suspension.

7. Reporting. Report misconduct through the platform. False reports are also a violation.

8. Admin Oversight. The ProxiCraft admin may suspend or permanently delete any account violating these terms.

9. Account Deletion. Users may delete their account at any time from their profile. All data is permanently removed.

10. Data and Privacy. Data is stored securely on Google Firebase and used only to operate the platform.
