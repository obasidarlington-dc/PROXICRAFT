📜 Terms and Conditions

1. Eligibility. Users must be at least 18 years of age and reside in Nigeria to register.

2. Artisan Qualification. All artisans must hold a minimum SSCE, WAEC, NECO, or equivalent qualification. False qualification information leads to immediate account suspension.

3. Role Restriction. Artisans may only apply for jobs matching their registered trade category.

4. Unique Identity. Each email address and username may be used for only one account. A person may not register as both artisan and client.

5. Accurate Information. Users must provide truthful information. Misrepresentation results in permanent account termination.

6. Conduct. Harassment, threats, or abusive behaviour results in immediate account suspension.

7. Reporting. Users who observe misconduct should report it through the platform. False reports are also a violation.

8. Admin Oversight. The ProxiCraft admin may suspend or permanently delete any account that violates these terms.

9. Account Deletion. Users may delete their accounts at any time from their profile. All data is permanently removed.

10. Data and Privacy. Personal data is stored securely and used only to operate the platform.

11. Limitation of Liability. ProxiCraft is a matching platform only and does not guarantee employment or quality of work.

12. Amendments. ProxiCraft may amend these terms at any time. Continued use constitutes acceptance.
